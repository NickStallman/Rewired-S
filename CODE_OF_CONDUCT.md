# Code of conduct

Be kind and be specific. This is a small project run in spare time by one person and improved by
people who are working on their own solar systems.

- Report problems with what you observed, what you expected and the diagnostics reference; do
  not assume bad faith when something is broken.
- Keep other people's data out of issues: serial numbers, plant names, addresses and Wi-Fi names
  do not belong here.
- Disagreements about approach are welcome; personal attacks, harassment and discrimination are
  not, and lead to removal from the project's spaces.

Concerns about conduct can be sent to nick@nickstallman.net.
